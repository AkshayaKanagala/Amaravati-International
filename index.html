Why we're building Community Assistant

Distance should never leave our parents helpless.

When children move to other towns, cities or countries, their parents may be left managing everyday needs alone. Our vision is to make finding local help as simple as making a phone call—without requiring app skills or technical knowledge.

Flagship initiative

Community Assistant: one call, a community that cares.

Our flagship product vision is a voice-first way for residents to request trusted local assistance without having to navigate complicated apps.

🛠️
Household helpPlumbers, electricians, housemaids, cooks, cleaners and drivers, using confirmed provider availability and resident approval.
🛒
Local grocery assistancePlanned inventory checks with participating stores, substitutions and confirmed fulfilment.
❤️
Safety-first medical guidanceA separate future module requiring medical, legal and operational review; emergencies must not wait for provider matching.

In development · Not accepting live bookings

How it is designed to work

Community first. Help nearby. Confirm clearly.

1. A resident calls and explains the need.

2. The platform checks verified providers who have confirmed availability for the requested time, starting within the community.

3. If necessary, matching expands to eligible providers within 2 km and then 5 km.

4. The provider accepts a specific request, and the resident explicitly confirms before booking.

Initial proof of concept: plumbers, one test community and simulated calls. Telugu/English voice support and broader services are planned later.

Concept and planning stage · Not yet available

The proposed experience

First understand. Then create.

1. A parent registers a student and chooses calling permissions.

2. The student calls to ask a question or request project guidance.

3. The AI explains the topic, checks understanding and supports syllabus-aligned practice where reliable materials are available.

4. Requested learning materials are prepared and delivered to the parent-approved email address.

Our long-term vision serves school, intermediate and university students. A smaller, parent-supervised pilot will be designed first. Features are proposed, not live services.
